# Sources and scope

Checked 27 October 2026. Prompts and interface copy are original, informed by these primary sources. No questionnaire, diagnostic scale, treatment protocol, or source artwork is redistributed.

- [NHS: thought record](https://www.nhs.uk/every-mind-matters/mental-wellbeing-tips/self-help-cbt-techniques/thought-record/). A structured reflection can explore a situation, feelings, thoughts, observations for and against, and another perspective. Room to Think implements these categories with optional re-rating.
- [NHS: reframing unhelpful thoughts](https://www.nhs.uk/every-mind-matters/mental-wellbeing-tips/self-help-cbt-techniques/reframing-unhelpful-thoughts/). The app invites evidence-based reflection without requiring a positive conclusion. A feeling may stay the same or increase.
- [NICE NG222: recommendations](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng222/chapter/recommendations). Clinical guided self-help involves structured material and support from a trained practitioner. This unsupervised app does not provide that support and is not an equivalent treatment. The small-action exercise is an ordinary planning/reflection prompt, not an individualized behavioural activation protocol.

No model generates responses. The project does not offer clinical triage, risk categorization, a symptom assessment, treatment personalization, or emergency monitoring. Its descriptive rating summaries cannot establish clinical benefit. Test records and any screenshots use invented examples.
